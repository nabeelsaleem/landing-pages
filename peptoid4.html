Support

Common
Questions.

Everything you need to know about our products, processes, and research standards.

Still have questions?

Our scientific team is available to discuss your specific research requirements and answer technical questions.

What are your peptides intended for?
All products sold by Symbiotic Peptides are strictly for laboratory research purposes only. They are not intended for human consumption, therapeutic use, or diagnostic applications. Purchasers must confirm research-use intent prior to order fulfillment.
Is a Certificate of Analysis (COA) provided?
Yes. We provide comprehensive analytical documentation — including HPLC purity analysis and mass spectrometry identity verification — accessible via our batch verification portal or upon request with your order confirmation.
How are products packaged and shipped?
Lyophilized peptides are packaged in sterile, tamper-evident glass vials under controlled atmosphere. Depending on compound stability requirements, climate-controlled expedited shipping options are utilized to ensure structural integrity upon arrival.
Do you offer custom peptide synthesis?
We accommodate custom synthesis for institutional laboratories, CROs, and biotech organizations. Please contact our scientific team directly to discuss sequencing, quantity, purity requirements, and turnaround timelines.
What is the typical shelf life of your peptides?
Lyophilized peptides stored at -20°C maintain full potency for 24-48 months. Once reconstituted, peptides should be used within 30 days when stored at 2-8°C. Specific stability data is available on each product's COA.
What payment methods do you accept?
We accept institutional purchase orders, ACH transfers, and major credit cards. Net-30 terms are available for established institutional accounts following credit verification.
Can I return a product if I'm not satisfied?
Due to the nature of our products, returns are accepted only for unopened vials with intact tamper-evident seals, within 14 days of delivery. Quality concerns with documented evidence are addressed through our quality assurance team on a case-by-case basis.
Do you ship internationally?
Yes, we ship to most countries with established research infrastructure. Import permits and customs documentation may be required depending on destination. Our logistics team coordinates all international shipments to ensure regulatory compliance.
