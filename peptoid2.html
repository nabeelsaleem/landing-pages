Support

Common Questions

What are your peptides intended for?
All products sold by Symbiotic Peptides are strictly for laboratory research purposes only. They are not intended for human consumption, therapeutic use, or diagnostic applications.
Is a Certificate of Analysis (COA) provided?
Yes. We provide comprehensive analytical documentation, including HPLC and MS reports, accessible via our batch verification portal or upon request with your order.
How are products packaged and shipped?
Lyophilized peptides are packaged securely in sterile glass vials. Depending on the fragility of the specific compound, climate-controlled shipping options are utilized to ensure structural integrity upon arrival.
Do you offer custom peptide synthesis?
We accommodate custom synthesis for institutional laboratories and CROs. Please contact our scientific team directly to discuss sequencing, quantity, and specific purity requirements.
