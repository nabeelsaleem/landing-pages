Support

Common Questions

What are your peptides intended for?
All products sold by Symbiotic Peptides are strictly for laboratory research purposes only. They are not intended for human consumption, therapeutic use, or diagnostic applications. By purchasing, you affirm that you are a qualified researcher or institution.
Is a Certificate of Analysis (COA) provided?
Yes. We provide comprehensive analytical documentation, including HPLC and MS reports, accessible via our batch verification portal or upon request with your order. Every batch is fully traceable from synthesis to final vial.
How are products packaged and shipped?
Lyophilized peptides are packaged securely in sterile, sealed glass vials. Depending on the fragility of the specific compound, climate-controlled shipping options are utilized to ensure structural integrity upon arrival. Same-day shipping available for orders placed before 2pm ET.
Do you offer custom peptide synthesis?
We accommodate custom synthesis for institutional laboratories and CROs. Please contact our scientific team directly to discuss sequencing, quantity, and specific purity requirements. Lead times typically range from 4–8 weeks depending on complexity.
What is your return and replacement policy?
Due to the sensitive nature of our compounds, all sales are final. However, if your order arrives compromised (broken seal, temperature excursion, analytical discrepancy), we will replace the batch at no cost. Documented evidence is required within 48 hours of receipt.
